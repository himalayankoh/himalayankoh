# Removed testimonial — retained for audit

This quotation was displayed on `/about` until 2026-10-11. It is retained here, not
displayed, so the decision is auditable and the customer's actual words are not
lost or silently softened.

**Verbatim as published:**

> My name is Darolyn Butler, and my ranch, Cypress Trails Equestrian Center, is near
> Humble, TX. I have a herd of 70 horses; they all work in my trail ride business and
> over half are also trained for endurance competition. All of the horses, considering
> our heat here in South Texas, need an excellent supply of salt, and I have been
> feeding it for about 15 months. I believe my horses are the healthier and able to
> thrive in this Texas heat due to this change to Himalayan Koh salt.

- **Name:** Darolyn Butler
- **Place as published:** Cypress Trails Equestrian Center, Humble, TX
- **Where it lived:** `src/views/AboutPage.tsx`, in the `testimonials` array
- **Removed:** 2026-10-11

## Why it came off the page

It attributes a health outcome — horses "the healthier and able to thrive" — to a
change of salt product. Even as a genuine customer's own belief, publishing it has
the site associate a salt product with a health result that no salt product can
substantiate. It is the same claim shape as the second-person sentences already
removed from the site's own copy (the hero, the pasture paragraph, the product
descriptions).

It was removed rather than reworded: editing a customer's sentence to soften it
would misquote them.

## The other testimonial was kept

James A. Brenek's was not removed. It comments on product quality and service —
"the best salt blocks we have purchased for our deer ranch" — and makes no
health-outcome claim. It is now published under a line stating these are customers'
own comments, not claims by Himalayan Koh.

## How to restore a version of this

If Darolyn Butler agrees to a factual version, one can be published — for example
ending at "…I have been feeding it for about 15 months." That requires her consent;
do not do it silently. The original text above is the record of what she actually
said.
