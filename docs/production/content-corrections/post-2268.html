Salt and trace minerals are two separate parts of a livestock nutrition program, and it is worth keeping them separate. This page covers the trace minerals dairy cows need from their diet. Himalayan rock salt's job is narrower than that, and it is described at the end.

Dairy cows require trace minerals such as copper, zinc, manganese, selenium, iodine and cobalt for essential physiological processes that affect their health and productivity.

Copper is needed for the formation of connective tissues, bone development, and red blood cell production. Zinc is important for immune function, skin and hoof health, and reproductive performance. Manganese plays a role in bone development, enzyme function, and reproduction. Selenium is a critical component of antioxidant enzymes that protect cells from damage, and is also important for immune function and reproductive performance. Iodine is necessary for the synthesis of thyroid hormones, which regulate metabolism, growth, and development. Cobalt is needed for the production of vitamin B12, which is important for energy metabolism.

A deficiency in any of these trace minerals can result in decreased milk production, impaired reproductive performance, reduced growth rates, and increased susceptibility to disease. Adequate levels are met through careful selection of feedstuffs, a mineral supplement balanced for your herd, and regular monitoring of herd health and productivity. A forage analysis is the practical starting point, because it shows what your pasture is actually short of.

Where salt fits

Himalayan rock salt supplies sodium and chloride, the two minerals every animal needs daily and the ones a salt block or loose salt is for. It is not a complete mineral supplement: it does not provide balanced copper, zinc, manganese, selenium, iodine or cobalt, and offering salt is not a substitute for a mineral program. For that reason salt and mineral are normally offered separately and free-choice, and the mineral product is chosen from a forage test rather than from a salt label.
