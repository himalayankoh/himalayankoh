## 1. Executive Summary & Market Context

In accordance with Ground Rule 8, **no freight rates have been invented or assumed**. Ocean freight figures are categorized into two distinct classes:
1. **Public Market Data (Index Benchmarks):** Publicly documented spot freight indices from Drewry WCI and Freightos FBX.
2. **Owner's Manual Quotes:** The operational quotations established by the business owner for Himalayan Koh's supply chain lanes.

### Market Realities on Global Container Shipping (September 2026):
- **Red Sea & Suez Diversions:** Geopolitical tensions and maritime security risks in the Red Sea require global carriers to reroute container traffic around the Cape of Good Hope for Asia/Pakistan-to-Europe and Asia/Pakistan-to-US East Coast routes. This adds 10–14 days of transit and introduces dynamic Emergency Bunker Surcharges (EBS) and War Risk Surcharges (WRS).
- **Public Index Coverage:** Global container indices (Drewry WCI, Freightos FBX, Platts) strictly monitor high-volume mainline routes (primarily China Shanghai/Ningbo/Yantian to US West Coast, US East Coast, and Northern Europe). **No independent public benchmark index publishes weekly spot rates for Pakistani origin ports (Karachi PKKHI / Port Qasim PKBQM)**. Rates for Pakistani ports are exclusively available via spot booking platforms (Maersk Spot, CMA CGM SpotOn, Hapag-Lloyd Quick Quotes) or forwarder contracts.

## 2. Methodology & Component Definitions

Every freight rate in this report states its precise component breakdown:
- **Included in Public Benchmark Rates:**
  - Base Ocean Freight (Port-to-Port transport).
  - Mandatory Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF) / Fuel Surcharge.
- **Excluded from Public Benchmark Rates (Must be priced separately in landed cost):**
  - Origin Terminal Handling Charges (OTHC / THC at loading port).
  - Destination Terminal Handling Charges (DTHC at discharge port).
  - Documentation, Bill of Lading (B/L) fees, Electronic Export Information (EEI/AES).
  - Origin customs export clearance & Destination import customs brokerage.
  - Inland drayage / container haulage from factory to port and port to distribution warehouse.
  - Peak Season Surcharges (PSS), Port Congestion Surcharges, Demurrage/Detention.
  - Marine cargo insurance (modeled separately in Himalayan Koh wholesale engine, typically 0.4%–0.5% CIF).

## 4. Requirements to Obtain Unavailable Commercial Rates

For all lanes marked `RATE UNAVAILABLE FROM PUBLIC SOURCE`, the following steps and credentials are required to convert them into usable figures:
1. **Direct Carrier Spot Contracts:**
   - **Maersk Spot API:** Himalayan Koh or its logistics partner must obtain a registered business account on Maersk.com to call Maersk's Instant Price API for PKKHI -> USNYC / USHOU / GBFXT.
   - **CMA CGM SpotOn / Hapag-Lloyd Quick Quotes:** Carrier spot tools provide fixed 24-hour rate locks for Pakistan and China shipments.
2. **Local Karachi Freight Forwarder Contracts:**
   - Port Qasim (PKBQM) and Karachi (PKKHI) require local freight forwarder agreements (e.g., Marshall Cargo, Ocean Bridge Pakistan, Expeditors Karachi) who provide:
     - All-in Ocean Freight.
     - Terminal Handling Charges (THC) at QICT / KICT.
     - Pakistan Customs Documentation and clearing agent fees.
3. **Freightos / WebCargo API Integration:**
   - As documented in `src/lib/wholesale/freightProvider.ts`, once an API key and secret are provisioned under Settings → "Ocean Freight — Live Rates", the app can query real-time market rates directly.
